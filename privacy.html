Privacy Policy

For the SnapFix website. Effective October 11, 2026.

This website doesn't ask you for anything, and it doesn't set cookies. Here is the full picture.

Who runs this site

This website is run by Oleksandr Rybitskyi, an individual developer, as the public page for SnapFix, an app that is still in development. It is not operated by a company.

What this site collects from you

Nothing you type or upload. There are no accounts, no sign-up forms, no newsletter, no comment boxes, and no photo uploads on this website. You can read every page without giving any information.

What happens automatically

  • Hosting logs. The site is hosted on Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel's servers may record standard request data such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages you requested, to keep the service running and secure. See Vercel's privacy policy for how they handle it.
  • Visit counts. The site uses Vercel Web Analytics to count page views. It doesn't use cookies, doesn't build a profile of you, and doesn't follow you to other sites. I see aggregate numbers like "how many people viewed the page this week," not who you are.

Cookies

This site doesn't set any cookies.

Third parties

Fonts and the 3D graphics library are served from this site itself, so opening the page doesn't send requests to Google Fonts or other content networks. Links to other websites, such as GitHub, are governed by those sites' own policies.

About the SnapFix app

This policy covers only this website. The SnapFix app itself will work with photos you take, so it will have its own privacy policy that explains how photos are processed and stored. That policy will be published before the app is available to the public.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect anything from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with an updated effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through GitHub.
