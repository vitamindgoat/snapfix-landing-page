One photo in. A clear answer out.

Most home problems are hard to describe and easy to photograph. SnapFix is built around that. You show it the problem the way you'd show a friend.

No forms to fill out. No technical terms to guess at. No self-diagnosing from forum threads. The AI does the first, confusing step so you can get straight to the right person.

Built by one person. Still being built.

SnapFix is my own project, and it's in active development. Parts of it work today and parts are still being improved. There's no download yet, no user count to show you, and no reviews, because it hasn't launched.

What's on this page is what the app is designed to do. When it's ready, this is where you'll find it.

How this page is made

3D models
The copper pipe and picket fence were modeled in Blender, with materials baked to textures. The light bulb and garden sprinkler are photogrammetry scans from Poly Haven, used under CC0.
Rendering
Everything you see moving is rendered live in your browser with Three.js. No video, no pre-rendered frames.
Type
Bricolage Grotesque for headlines, Schibsted Grotesk for text. Both self-hosted.
Code
Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, no framework and no build step. Hosted on Vercel. Source on GitHub.
Inspiration
Layout, pacing and motion references came from studying the showcases on Godly and Awwwards.
